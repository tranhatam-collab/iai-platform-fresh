## 3. LANGUAGE PRINCIPLES

### 3.1 Vietnamese (Primary)
Vietnamese is the primary language system.

It must be:
- fully accented
- calm
- precise
- non-promotional
- structurally clear
- conceptually stable

Mandatory rules:
- use full diacritics
- no slang
- no texting abbreviations
- target sentence length: 10–22 words
- target paragraph length: 2–4 sentences
- prefer direct meaning over decorative phrasing
- do not mix English terms unless they are technical identifiers

### 3.2 English (Secondary)
English is the secondary language system.

It must be:
- strategic
- architectural
- globally readable
- role-aware
- definition-based

Mandatory rules:
- prefer noun-based phrasing
- prefer short declarative sentences
- avoid startup clichés
- avoid emotional persuasion
- do not translate Vietnamese literally when it weakens meaning

## 7. PAGE STRUCTURE RULE

Every page must follow:

- H1: one only
- Subline: 1–2 sentences
- H2: 3–6 sections
- Paragraph: 2–4 sentences
- CTA: directional only, not sales-driven

Every page must answer:
1. What is this surface?
2. What does it do?
3. What does it not do?
4. What should the user do next?
