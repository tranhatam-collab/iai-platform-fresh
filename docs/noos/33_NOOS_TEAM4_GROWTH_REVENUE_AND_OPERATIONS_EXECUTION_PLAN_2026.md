## 7. KPI contract Team 4

### Core KPIs
- conversion rate by product
- AOV
- checkout completion rate

- upgrade rate (Entry->Core, Core->Master, Master->Team)

### Health KPIs
- support response SLA
- refund/dispute rate
- pricing mismatch incidents
- failed-fulfillment incidents

## 8. Growth guardrails (không được vi phạm)

Cho phép:
- authority-led messaging
- structured bundle comparison
- upgrade-credit clarity
- English-first international SEO and Vietnamese local entry pages with full diacritics

Không cho phép:
- fake scarcity
- discount spam
- loud promo tone
- mâu thuẫn price/license between page and checkout
- mixed-language public pages used as SEO landing surfaces
- copy không dấu hoặc sai locale trên surfaces public
